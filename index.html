About Us

Your neighbourhood design studio

Sakhi Designers creates custom-stitched womenswear — partywear frocks, georgette and ethnic designs, kidswear, blouses and lehengas — with every piece made to order.

From half-saree ceremony launches to everyday ethnic wear, our two Kukatpally branches — Gopal Nagar and Pragathi Nagar — serve customers who want designs tailored to them, not picked off a rack. Message us on WhatsApp to share your design idea.
